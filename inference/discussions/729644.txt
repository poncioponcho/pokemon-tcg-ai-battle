Are top agents mostly heuristic, or are people successfully using RL/BC?

Hi everyone,

I’m curious about what kinds of agents people are currently using in this competition.

My current agent is mainly rule-based/heuristic, with some matchup-specific routing and planning. Its leaderboard rating is usually around the 850-900 range, although the score can fluctuate quite a bit.

I have experimented with behavior cloning and rollout-based plan selection, but so far their offline improvements have not transferred reliably to the live leaderboard. I’m wondering whether higher-rated agents—especially those around 1000–1100—are mainly based on:

Hand-written heuristics

Search or simulation

Behavior cloning / imitation learning

Reinforcement learning or self-play

A hybrid of these approaches

If you are comfortable sharing, I would also be interested in:

Your approximate rating range

Whether your method performs consistently online

Whether BC or RL actually improved over a strong heuristic baseline

How much search you can realistically run within the time limit

Whether matchup-specific routing has been useful

There is no need to reveal code, deck lists, or private strategy details. Broad approach categories and approximate rating bands would already be very helpful.

Thanks! I’d love to understand what approaches are working for others and whether strong heuristics are still competitive with learned policies.
